Reiki

Rest, balance & quiet reflection

A gentle practice for rest, balance, and quiet reflection

Reiki is a soft, energy-based wellness practice that invites deep relaxation and a feeling of balance. A Reiki session at Tarasana is a chance to slow down, settle in, and give yourself permission to simply be.

You stay fully clothed while Tara rests her hands lightly on, or just above, different areas of the body. Nothing is manipulated or stretched, and you don't need to take part in any particular way.

Just get comfortable, relax, and let yourself receive.

What is Reiki?

Reiki uses light touch, or hands held just above the body, to create a calm, restorative experience.

You might notice warmth or tingling, or you might simply drift into deep relaxation. Everyone's experience is different, and there is nothing in particular you need to feel or achieve.

There's nothing you need to do or believe, either. Reiki is simply a quiet space to turn your attention inward and step back from the demands of the day.

What to expect

Each session is meant to feel comfortable and peaceful from start to finish. You will:

  • Stay fully clothed
  • Settle in somewhere quiet and comfortable
  • Rest while Tara's hands sit lightly on, or just above, different areas of the body
  • Have time to slow down and gather your attention
  • Simply rest — no need to actively participate

Some people feel warmth, tingling, or other sensations; others just feel calm and still. There's no right or wrong way to experience Reiki.

Reiki for real life

Between work, responsibilities, relationships, and everything else pulling at your attention, there's often very little room for stillness — and pausing on purpose can feel unfamiliar.

A Reiki session makes room for exactly that.

Instead of asking you to accomplish anything, it invites you to slow down and notice the present moment: let your body settle, quiet your mind, and spend a little time focused inward.

Reiki and the Tarasana approach

Tarasana is built on simple, mindful tools that create meaningful shifts in how we move, breathe, and live.

Reiki belongs naturally in that philosophy. Like gentle yoga and vibroacoustic therapy, it offers a chance to step away from the noise of everyday life and reconnect with yourself.

There's no performance and no expectation. You don't need to be flexible, experienced, or familiar with energy work — only willing to slow down and receive.
