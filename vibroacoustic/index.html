Vibroacoustic Therapy

Touchless meditative massage

Vibroacoustic Therapy (VAT) is a holistic wellness approach that combines low-frequency sound vibrations with specially composed therapeutic music. You recline on a vibroacoustic bed equipped with embedded tactile transducers while using headphones, creating a calming, whole-body experience.

It is sound you feel as much as hear: the bed carries low frequencies through the body in slow waves and pulses, while the headphones hold the fuller piece around you — instruments, tones, and the natural world. There is nothing to do once you lie down.

What it is

VAT uses gentle vibrations (often in the 20–120 Hz range) transmitted through the body's water-rich tissues to encourage relaxation, release tension, and restore balance.

Benefits & support areas

  • Stress & anxiety support
  • Pain & discomfort support
  • Muscle tension & spasticity support
  • Sleep disturbances support
  • Mood & emotional well-being
  • Mental clarity & focus

What to expect

  • Settle: rest fully clothed on the bed and put on headphones (eye mask optional)
  • Receive: low frequencies move through the body while the music unfolds around you
  • Return: the session closes gently, with time to come back at your own pace
  • Choose from eight sound journeys or a brainwave session, or let us choose for you based on your needs
  • Sessions last 30, 45, or 60 minutes

Eight sound journeys

Every session follows one of eight journeys, each composed for a different intention. Choose the one that speaks to you, or let us choose for you based on your needs — there's no wrong pick.

  1. Stillness — Stress & anxiety relief
  2. Clear Mind — Mental cleanse & clarity
  3. Ease — Pain relief & comfort
  4. Deep Rest — Deep sleep & restoration
  5. Renewal — Aura & whole-body cleanse
  6. Open Heart — Emotional healing & heart opening
  7. Alignment — Full chakra alignment
  8. Abundance — Abundance & manifestation

Journey 01

Stillness

Stress & anxiety relief Theta

A slow, cinematic drift for a nervous system that has been running too long.

Long, evolving chords unfold with almost no melody to follow, so the thinking mind has nothing to hold onto and finally lets go. Beneath it all, a slow heartbeat pulse moves through the body like a tide.
